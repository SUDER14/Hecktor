Project status, 11 Nov 2026

Where the real HECKTOR work stands. Every line comes from a checked report; nothing here is a result. No model has been trained yet.

  • Done
    Data audited
    782 cases from 8 centres, CT, PET and label on one grid. Spacing in-plane 0.49 to 2.73 mm, through-plane 1.0 to 5.0 mm. Labels hold values 0, 1 and 2; their meaning is not confirmed.
  • Done
    Cropped cache built
    782 files, 43.6 GiB, head-and-neck crop of 350 by 300 mm chosen from the CT. All 782 labels sit inside the crop. Five spot-checked cases match the source files exactly.
  • Done
    Split fixed
    592 train, 67 validation, 123 test (CHUS and CHUV held out). 77 of the 123 test cases have a spacing pair never seen in training, which makes the test harder and fits the question.
  • Done
    PET scale rule
    52 cases have PET values hundreds of times larger than the rest and the units are not stated. Every case is normalised by its own 99.5th percentile; none is excluded.
  • Waiting
    GPU for training
    No GPU on the working machine. A VIT GPU is being requested and may take about a month. Cloud GPUs are blocked until the data provider agrees in writing, because the licence limits use to the VIT site.
  • Waiting
    Answers from the organisers
    Label meaning, PET units, cloud use, image re-publication.
  • Not started
    Baselines B1 to B5, nnU-Net, ablations
    Timing and VRAM are unmeasured. The correctness checks come first.
  • Not started
    Results, statistics, thesis chapters
    The analysis plan will be committed before any test number is seen.

What the network is told about spacing

The spacing of this scan becomes a three-number vector. A small hypernetwork turns it into a scale (γ) and a shift (β) for the feature channels. Change the spacing in the bar above and these move.

Hypernetwork weights

The three image pairs use this slice as a stand-in for a feature map, and show F before and after F′ = γ · F + β. The z-score statistics are placeholders; the real ones come from training scans only. At the start of training γ = 1 and β = 0, so before and after are identical.

Segmentation and doubt

Per voxel, the head outputs evidence for background and tumour. Little total evidence means high uncertainty. Click the image to inspect a voxel, or move the sliders to see how the probability curve changes.

The curve is the Beta density that a two-class Dirichlet gives for the tumour probability. Narrow means the model is sure of its own probability. Wide means it is not.

Research prototype, not for clinical use. The phantom is generated in your browser from simple shapes. No scan leaves or enters this page, and a scan you load is read locally and is not stored or sent anywhere.

HECKTOR data is used under a licence that limits it to the VIT site and bars sharing, so this page never contains or uploads patient images. The phantom stands in for real cases.

In the HECKTOR 2026 files the PET is already resampled onto the CT grid, so this page expects CT, PET and label on one grid. Loaded volumes are reduced to at most 256 voxels in-plane to stay responsive.
