Research prototype, not for clinical use. The phantom is generated in your browser from simple shapes.

HECKTOR data is used under a licence that limits it to the VIT site and bars sharing. This page is served on 127.0.0.1 only, contacts no other host, and has no control that exports or saves an image.

Real cases are read from the cropped cache: CT, PET and label on one grid. PET is shown divided by the case's own scale (SUV-like, units not stated). Volumes are reduced to at most 256 voxels in-plane for display; slices are not reduced.
