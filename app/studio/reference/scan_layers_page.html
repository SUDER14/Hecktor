Research prototype, not for clinical use. The phantom is built in your browser. A scan you load is read locally; nothing is uploaded or stored.

Layers are evenly spaced slices of the real volume, at most 24 of them. PET is auto-scaled to its own brightest 0.3 percent, because the released PET is not on one unit scale. Loaded volumes are reduced to at most 256 voxels in-plane.
